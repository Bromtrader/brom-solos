    01 · About

    Nobody taught me to build.
    I taught myself to obsess.

    I'm BROM — a frontend developer based in Kenya who learned to code from documentation, YouTube tabs left open at 2am, and a refusal to ship anything that felt average. No bootcamp, no degree in it. Just a browser, a text editor, and an inbox full of "why isn't this centering."

    What started as curiosity about how websites move and glow turned into a discipline: every layout gets questioned, every animation earns its place, every project has to feel like it was made on purpose. I care about the 2% most developers skip — the easing curve, the hover state, the loading screen nobody asked for but everyone notices.

    My goal isn't just to write code that works. It's to build interfaces good enough that people stop scrolling and ask who made this.

    04 · Journey

    Self-taught, milestone by milestone.

    🌱

    Foundations

    Learned semantic HTML and CSS layout systems (Flexbox, Grid) by rebuilding real websites from scratch, just to understand how they worked.

    ⚙️

    Interactivity

    Picked up vanilla JavaScript, DOM manipulation, and event-driven UI — then started reaching for animation libraries to bring designs to life.

    First Client

    Delivered Uncle Ratt Wines & Spirits — my first real commercial project — and learned what it means to design for someone else's business, not just a portfolio piece.

    Started slowing down on purpose — studying Awwwards-level sites, motion timing, and accessibility — to build work that holds up under scrutiny.
